Privacy Policy — Hanzi Practice

Effective: 2026-09-14

Applies to: the Hanzi Practice web app at hanzipractice.app and the Hanzi Practice Android app.

Summary

  • We do not collect, store, or sell personal data.
    • We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no ads.
      • Your study progress stays on your device unless you choose to enable GitHub Gist sync.

      Data stored on your device

      Hanzi Practice keeps your progress in your browser's local storage on your own device: words marked known, handwriting practice counts and saved free writes, activity log, daily goal, reminder setting, and pen-only setting. This data never leaves your device unless you export it or enable sync. You can delete it at any time with the in-app Reset, by clearing site data in your browser, or by uninstalling the app.

      Optional GitHub Gist sync

      If you enable sync, you provide your own GitHub personal access token. The app stores that token only on your device and uses it to read and write a private Gist in your own GitHub account containing your progress. We never see the token or the Gist. GitHub's privacy policy governs that data: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement. Turning sync off and clearing the token stops all communication with GitHub.

      Network requests the app makes

      • hanzipractice.app: loading and updating the app itself.
        • api.github.com: only when Gist sync is enabled.
          • cdn.jsdelivr.net: stroke data for the rare character not bundled with the app. The request contains only the character.
            • mdbg.net: opens in your browser only when you tap the dictionary link.

            None of these requests include your identity, and we do not operate any of these services except hanzipractice.app, which is static hosting with no server-side logging by us.

            Notifications

            The daily reminder is optional. It runs locally on your device and requires your permission. No notification data is sent anywhere.

            Children

            The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

            Permissions (Android)

            Internet: to load the app and optional sync. Notifications: only if you turn on the daily reminder.

            Changes

            Updates to this policy will be posted at hanzipractice.app/privacy with a new effective date.
